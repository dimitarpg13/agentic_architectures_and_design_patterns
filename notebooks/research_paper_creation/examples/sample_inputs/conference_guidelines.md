# Conference Submission Guidelines (Generic ML Conference)

- Maximum 9 pages of content (excluding references and appendix)
- Abstract should not exceed 250 words
- Sections should follow: Abstract, Introduction, Related Work, Methodology, Experiments, Conclusion
- All claims must be supported by experimental evidence or theoretical proofs
- Include comparison with at least 3 established baselines
- Report standard deviation or confidence intervals where applicable
- Figures and tables should be self-contained with descriptive captions
